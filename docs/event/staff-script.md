# Staff scripts

## 15-second introduction (inviting a visitor)

> "Hi! Want to help wiTTy rescue some government translations? It's a two-minute game: three quick missions, sorting requests, spotting inconsistent terms, and getting an announcement out on time. No sign-up needed. Just tap to play!"

## After the game (about 30 s, adapt to their power-up pick)

| Their pick / interest | Talking point |
|---|---|
| Web portal | "SG Translate Together is the portal for everyday translation: letters, notices, short documents." |
| Website translation | "Polyglot helps make government websites available in multiple languages." |
| API | "The SGT 2.0 API lets your digital service or chatbot translate inside your own system." |
| Glossary | "Consistent government terms are a big part of what SGT 2.0 is designed for. Ask us how glossaries work." |
| None / not sure | "No problem! What does translation look like in your team today?" |

Always add: *"AI translations may still need human review before publication."* Don't make accuracy claims.

## Closing

> "Thanks for playing! If it's useful for your work, the QR on screen takes you to our registration form. Let me get your GovEntry stamp sorted."

(Staff: Shift+S → *Show GovEntry stamp reminder*, then follow the official GovEntry scanning process.)
